## How to read this

- **Owner's rule:** the sections of the owner's text, grouped where they ask the same thing.
- **Already stated in:** the builder texts. These are the [builder constitution](../builder/claude-code-builder-constitution.md) (Rules), the [checkpoint and verification rule](../builder/checkpoint-and-verification-rule.md), the [master execution constitution](../builder/master-execution-constitution.md) (§), the [directive on verification and platform independence](../builder/verification-and-platform-independence-directive.md), the [quality directive](../builder/quality-consistency-and-correction-directive.md), whose sections are given in parentheses after its name, and, from DEC-051 on, [owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md) (OD9 Rules).
- **Applied through:** the existing mechanism or decision record that applies the rule.
- **Gap and handling:** a gap is a rule that no builder text states and no mechanism applies yet, or a mechanism not yet built. Its handling names where the gap is closed.
- Some rules list the fields of a record, such as an exception's or a technical-debt item's. Those fields are stated only in the owner's text, and they apply through the decision record that keeps that text in force (DEC-051, decision 3).

## Result

- **No conflict.** No rule of these rule sets contradicts a builder text. Each restates, extends, or sets the fields of rules the builder texts already have. Where they differ, the stricter applies (DEC-033).
- **No real gap calls for another builder text.** The gaps are handled in the existing mechanisms or the steps already scheduled. Loading these rule sets in full beside the builder texts would state most of their rules twice. That is what owner decisions 9 (Rules 3 and 16) and owner decisions 17 ("THE OBJECTIVE IS NOT MORE RULES.") ask the project not to do.
- **They stay in force** through their decision records, and `CLAUDE.md` names them. That is [DEC-051](../decisions/DEC-051-adopt-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md), decision 3.
- **Owner decisions 22 (2026-10-27) changes none of this.** Its two rule sets contradict no builder text either, and each of their rules is stated in one or applied through an existing mechanism, except the last known good configuration, which waits for the stage that first reloads one. They stay in force through [DEC-054](../decisions/DEC-054-configuration-size-limit-and-retired-backup-branches.md) (decision 5), the same way.
