**The image's license.** TimescaleDB is under the Apache License 2.0 outside its `tsl` directory, and under the Timescale License inside it ([the repository's license file at 2.32.0](https://github.com/timescale/timescaledb/blob/2.32.0/LICENSE)). ## Checkpoint B: U5, validation, freshness, quality, quarantine, and clock drift

**Validation** (`atp.marketdata`; MKD-001, MKD-003, MKD-004, MKD-008, MKD-009, MKD-011, MKD-013; SR-12, SR-13). `MarketDataValidator` checks each item an adapter gives, once, in MKD-003's order, and the first check the item fails quarantines it with that cause: its venue's clock drifting (CLOCK_PROBLEM); a receipt after the time of the check, or an event time, a bar's opening, or a closed bar's end later than its receipt by more than the limit (INVALID_TIMESTAMP); a ticker without any price, or a trading market's metadata without its increments (MISSING_FIELD); an impossible value (IMPOSSIBLE_PRICE): a price, increment, or contract size not above zero, a quantity, volume, or minimum below zero, a trade or a snapshot's level of zero quantity, a ticker or snapshot whose best bid is not below its best ask, a snapshot's levels out of order, or a bar whose high is below its open or close or whose low is above either; an evidence identifier among the last ones seen, or a book update the market's book already holds (DUPLICATE_EVENT); a book update that does not follow the market's book by the venue's sequence numbers, the book then broken until a fresh snapshot (SEQUENCE_GAP); a price beyond the outlier limit from its market's and stream's reference price, until a run of outliers confirms the move (OUTLIER); and a price beyond the inconsistency limit from every other venue's valid latest price for the same market and stream, when enough venues give one (EXCHANGE_INCONSISTENCY). A quarantined item becomes a quarantine record with its cause and evidence and is never passed on (MKD-009); an item that passes is assessed and returned as `Validated`, the only form in which consumers accept market data, eligible for use only if VALID when assessed (MKD-012): `eligible` keeps that assessment, so an item used later is assessed again at the time of use (`assess`), by its consumers (U7 onward). `deduplicate` keeps the first item of each evidence identifier, and applied again changes nothing; `in_order` gives one order for the same items, however they arrived. Prices are compared exactly, as integer ratios, without division.

**Freshness and quality** (MKD-006, MKD-008, MKD-012; D3). An item's age runs from the time it describes, the venue's event time, a closed bar's end where the venue gives no event time, or else its receipt; against its stream's limits it is VALID, then STALE, then INVALID, and a stream with no validated item is INVALID (`StreamFreshness`). The quality score, rule 1.0, is 100, 50, or 0 by freshness, 10 less when the venue gave no time for the item, never below 0. Each assessment and quarantine record names the version of its rule, and each validated item the version of the validation it passed (MKD-010).

**Clock drift** (`atp.health`, SYS-29; HLT-013, HLT-014; D3; SR-14). `ClockMonitor` keeps each venue's latest server time, read with the request's sending and the answer's receipt, and its latest event time. A server time bounds the venue's offset from the local clock on both sides, so it can show the venue ahead, behind, or in time; an event time bounds it from below only, so it can show the venue ahead only. When some venue is in time, the local clock is in sync and each drifting venue's data gets the cause CLOCK_PROBLEM; when none is and at least `minimum_venues` venues drift the same way, the local clock is the one drifting: cross-system delays are unreliable, and no venue's data is quarantined; otherwise the local clock is undetermined, delays are unreliable, and each drifting venue's data is quarantined. A drift found raises DATA DEGRADED, kept for SYS-29's health state (CORE TRADING FOUNDATION); alerts come with SYS-28 (SR-14).

## Checkpoint B: U4, the parts that need no venue's data

**The interface** (`atp.exchange`; EXA-001, EXA-004, EXA-006, EXA-011, EXA-013). `VenueAdapter` is what each venue's adapter gives the rest of the platform, in Stage 2 without a credential: the venue's server time, with the local times its request was sent and its answer received, for SYS-29's clock monitor (HLT-013); its markets, with their increments and minimums (ARCH-011); a market's ticker, recent trades, book snapshot, bars, and funding rate; the venue's state; and a stream of markets, kept until the venue refuses the region or the task is cancelled. Each request's result is an outcome; an answer's content is a translation: the canonical items of SYS-02's contracts and, for each part that could not be translated, its record with the cause, for SYS-02 (EXA-013). The adapters come venue by venue, each after its verification (D1; OQ-34).

**Messages and requests** (EXA-013, EXA-014, EXA-015, EXA-007; ARCH-042; D5). Every message is checked against its size limit before any of it is read, and decoded so that each JSON number keeps the text the venue wrote (`JsonNumber`). A message too large, or not UTF-8 JSON (a name given twice in one object, NaN or Infinity, or nesting too deep for the decoder included), is untranslatable input with its cause, OVERSIZED or MALFORMED, recorded by its content's sha256 digest and length, never the content. A request's result, as its transport reports it, is SUCCESS with the body of a complete 2xx answer; FAILURE for a rate-limit refusal (429, or 418), with the wait the venue asked for, a region refusal (451), another 4xx, or a request never sent (its name unresolved, its connection refused, or the venue's certificate failing TLS verification); TIMEOUT when no complete answer came in time; and UNKNOWN for any other status, or a connection broken after the request was sent. An adapter may refine a failure's cause from the venue's own signals; it never turns a TIMEOUT or an UNKNOWN into a SUCCESS.

**Rate limits** (EXA-014). Each of a venue's limits, REST or WebSocket, for the whole venue or one endpoint, is a `RateLimit`: at most so much weight in any sliding window. A request names the limits it counts against, with its weight in each; `RequestQueue` admits waiting requests one at a time, in priority order, each only when every limit it names has room, a request that arrives while the queue waits for its first request's room waiting until that wait ends, and records its weights as it admits it, so no request goes over a limit, also under concurrency; should its clock or wait fail, each waiting request is given the failure. A rate-limit refusal pauses every request to the venue for its backoff, doubling with each refusal in a row, or longer when the venue asks. `retry_read` sends a read again only after a TIMEOUT, an UNKNOWN, a rate-limit refusal, or a connection that failed, after the backoff or the wait the venue asked for, whichever is longer, and not when the venue asks for longer than the backoff's longest wait, which leaves its caller to decide; a region refusal is never retried, and no other route is tried (EXA-007). The retry rule for financial actions comes with them in CORE TRADING FOUNDATION (EXE-008).

**Connection resilience** (EXA-015, EXA-007). A venue's `VenueHealth` keeps its state, U2's `VenueStatus` with its `UnavailableCause`, states of their own. A circuit breaker opens after failures in a row, a timeout, an UNKNOWN, or a connection that failed, refuses every request while open, then lets one trial through at a time, while each trial ends within the open time, whose success closes it and whose failure opens it again; a rate-limit refusal, or a refusal of one request as it was sent, is neither a success nor a failure and leaves the circuit as it is, and a trial that ends so, or with no outcome, lets another request through once it has run for the open time. Outcomes are not tied to requests: a success or a failure recorded while a trial runs is taken as the trial's. A region refusal makes the venue unavailable for good. `maintain_connection` keeps a stream connected: it reconnects after a drop, and after a silence longer than the heartbeat timeout, which marks the feed stale, with a backoff between failed attempts, or the wait the venue asked for when it refused a connection and that is longer; a connection counts as a success only when its first message arrives.

**The limits.** `Backoff`, `RateLimit`, and `ResilienceLimits` are contracts without defaults, their schemas committed: IMPLEMENTATION CHOICE values that each venue's adapter gives, from the venue's published limits; none is set yet, so no setting is added and MIG-034 asks for no record yet.

**The structure test** (TEC-005; EXA-004, EXA-007, EXA-011). CCXT may be imported only in `atp.exchange`; no place there gives a value to one of CCXT's credential names, those of its `requiredCredentials` in the locked version (`apiKey`, `secret`, `password`, `uid`, `accountId`, `login`, `twofa`, `privateKey`, `walletAddress`, `token`), as a keyword argument, a key of a dict literal, `setattr`'s name, or an assigned attribute or item, also by unpacking or as a `for`, `with`, or comprehension target; nothing in `src/` gives a TLS switch (`verify`, CCXT's `validateServerSsl` and `ssl_context`, `ssl`, `verify_ssl`, `check_hostname`, `verify_mode`, `cert_reqs`, urllib3's `assert_hostname`) a value that turns it off, a false constant other than `None` (requests reads any false `verify` as off) or urllib3's `"NONE"`, in those ways, as an assigned name, also by `:=`, or as a parameter's default, or uses a name that turns verification off (`CERT_NONE`, `_create_unverified_context`, `_create_stdlib_context`, `PYTHONHTTPSVERIFY`, and `CLIENT_AUTH`, the purpose of a server's context), or makes an `SSLContext` with no protocol, or one other than `PROTOCOL_TLS_CLIENT`, each such context verifying no certificate; and `atp.exchange` imports only `atp.contracts` and `atp.marketdata` (D4). Each rule has planted samples that the test must find. A name or value computed when the code runs, such as a name given to `setattr` in a variable, or a switch given a variable, an expression, or a call's result, is beyond a rule that reads the code, as is a credential given to a CCXT object outside `atp.exchange` or in a venue's own header; review guards them (Gate 3's I-28 and I-30).

**Tests:** 160 of `atp.exchange` (`tests/exchange/`): 26 of messages, 53 of requests' outcomes, 39 of the rate-limit manager, 33 of connection resilience, and 9 of the interface; and 82 cases of the structure test: its rules for CCXT, credentials, and TLS, with their planted samples, and `atp.exchange`'s imports.

## Checkpoint C: U7, the Quantitative Engine with the Fee and Slippage Engines

Built as checkpoint C's first unit, before checkpoint B's venue adapters ([DEC-080](../decisions/DEC-080-checkpoint-c-before-the-venue-adapters.md)), in `atp.quant` (SYS-03), with division in `atp.numeric`; what each calculation computes is in the [Quantitative Engine's specification](../systems/quantitative-engine.md), "Stage 2's calculations", and in its docstring.

**Division** (ARCH-010, TEC-003; the [numerical policy](../architecture/numerical-policy.md), N4 and N5). `divide(dividend, divisor, increment, rounding)` gives a quotient rounded to a multiple of an increment in a named direction, from the exact quotient: the whole number of increments and the remainder come from `divmod` of the dividend by the divisor times the increment, the direction taking the quotient's sign from both operands. `quantize` and `divide` share one implementation of the directions; `quantize`'s behaviour and its tests are unchanged.

**The calculations** (QNT-001 to QNT-007, Stage 2's parts). Spread and depth within a band of the mid, from a ticker or a book snapshot; the Fee Engine's fee at a venue's configured schedule (D8) and its funding payment; the Slippage Engine's walk of a taker order through a book snapshot; and indicators of a market's closed bars, in floats. Exact results are amounts; a quotient is rounded by `divide` in the direction that never understates a cost: the relative spread up, a buy's average price up, a sell's down. Each takes market data only as `Validated` that was VALID when validated and is VALID at the time of use, which its caller gives with the limits (MKD-012; the item carried to this unit, below); a calculation without a result is refused with its cause (`CalculationRefused`), never given as zero.

**Results** (MKD-005, MKD-010; ARCH-025). Seven contracts, their schemas committed: `atp.quant.spread`, `.depth`, `.slippage`, `.fee`, `.funding`, `.indicator`, and `.correlation`. Each carries the time it describes, its inputs' evidence identifiers, its calculation's version, and its own evidence identifier, a sha256 digest of the form `atp.calculation/1`: the calculation's name and version, its parameters, and its inputs. `atp.marketdata` now exports `item_time_ns` and `bar_end_ns`, the rule of the time an item describes, so that no second copy of it is written.

**The structure test** (TEC-003; D5). `FLOAT_ALLOWED` holds `atp.quant._indicators`, the one module that may use floats, and a new rule refuses its importing anything from `atp.numeric`, under any name or path, so that it builds no amount; `atp.quant` may import `atp.config`, `atp.contracts`, `atp.marketdata`, and `atp.numeric` (D4).

**Tests:** of `atp.quant` (`tests/quant/`), the exact calculations against hand-worked values, with properties for the walk (never better than the best, exact, repeatable) and the fee (growing with the notional), and the indicators against values worked by hand from their definitions; of `divide` (`tests/numeric/test_division.py`), with its properties against exact fractions; and the structure test's new rule with its planted samples. Their counts are in the [checkpoint C (U7) verification](stage-02-checkpoint-c-u7-verification.md)'s "Checks".
