> Where every section of [Handoff Part 2](../handoffs/part-2-consolidated-additional-systems.md) went. The "New requirements" column is generated by [`tools/docs/build_index.py`](../../tools/docs/README.md) from the requirement sources (`P2§N`); the last column is written by hand and checked (every ID in it must exist). Decisions: [DEC-024](../decisions/DEC-024-part-2-reconciliation.md). Verification and the documentation audit: [Part 2 verification record](part-2-verification.md).

## How to read this

- **New requirements:** requirements whose source cites this section. They live in their owning specification; the [registry](../requirements/registry.md) indexes them.
- **Existing coverage, findings, notes:**
  - "Covered by" means the section adds nothing new and is fully met by the listed requirements.
  - "Also" lists existing requirements the new ones extend. They are cross-referenced, never copied (P2§185).
  - "Restates" marks a later section that repeats an earlier one (DUP-31).
  - Process sections say where they were applied.
- Nothing in Part 2 was dropped. A section is either mapped to requirements, recorded as a finding (CF-14, OQ-24 to OQ-26, TC-07, all since decided by the owner in DEC-026 to DEC-030), or applied as process. Classification follows the wording ([DEC-024](../decisions/DEC-024-part-2-reconciliation.md), decision 1).
