# Handoff Part 3 Reconciliation

> Where every section of [Handoff Part 3](../handoffs/part-3-consolidated-autonomy-capital-scaling.md) went. The "New requirements" column is generated by [`tools/docs/build_index.py`](../../tools/docs/README.md) from the requirement sources (`P3§N`); the last column is written by hand and checked (every ID in it must exist). Decisions: [DEC-031](../decisions/DEC-031-part-3-reconciliation.md). Verification: [Part 3 verification record](part-3-verification.md).

## How to read this

- **New requirements:** requirements whose source cites this section. They live in their owning specification; the [registry](../requirements/registry.md) indexes them.
- **Existing coverage, findings, notes:**
  - "Covered by" means the section adds nothing new and is fully met by the listed requirements.
  - "Also" lists existing requirements the new ones extend. They are cross-referenced, never copied.
  - "Rule SR-nn" points to the section's entry in the [System Rules Register](../requirements/system-rules-register.md).
  - Process sections say where they were applied.
- Nothing in Part 3 was dropped. A section is either mapped to requirements, recorded as a finding (CF-17, CF-18, DUP-32 to DUP-38, OQ-27, TC-08, TC-09; CF-17, CF-18, OQ-27, and TC-08 since decided by the owner in [DEC-035](../decisions/DEC-035-owner-decisions-part-3-findings.md), which also confirmed DUP-34 and DUP-35 and approved this reconciliation), or applied as process. Classification follows the wording ([DEC-031](../decisions/DEC-031-part-3-reconciliation.md), decision 1).
