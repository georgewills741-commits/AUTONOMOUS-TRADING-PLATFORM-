- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decided by:** the owner: the approval to proceed to Stage 2 planning, and the three answers with their conditions ([owner decisions 34](../handoffs/owner-decisions-34-approval-to-plan-stage-2-and-answers-on-the-directive.md)). At 02:49 UTC the builder asked the owner four questions together (the [quality directive](../builder/quality-consistency-and-correction-directive.md)'s §11): the approval to proceed to Stage 2 planning (X8 of the [stage record](../traceability/stage-01-foundation.md)'s exit checklist); how the daily figure of about 1.2% net of [owner decisions 29](../handoffs/owner-decisions-29-universal-opportunity-intelligence-and-profit-optimization.md) reads beside PLT-008 and TNP-014; what its "Maker-versus-taker execution decisions" approve of adaptive execution (EXE-011), FUTURE until the owner approves it ([DEC-027](DEC-027-part-2-open-questions.md)); and whether the certificate's backup branch may be kept with the twelve retired ones if its deletion is refused.
- **The answers**, at 04:31 UTC, each the option the builder recommended: "Approve Stage 2 planning"; "Measurement only"; "Only maker-or-taker"; "Keep it with the others".
- **The message.** At 07:04 UTC (queued at 07:02 UTC) the owner confirmed the second, third, and fourth answers in writing and gave their conditions, and asked the builder to continue the authorized Stage 2 planning process under the three gates and the repository's rules. Among its words: "Before making changes, inspect the existing canonical requirements. Reuse and reinforce what already exists. - **The backup branch.** The certificate's change was backed up on `claude/unverified-backup-x6`. Carrying a retired branch past Stage 1 needs the owner's explicit, recorded approval ([DEC-044](DEC-044-preserve-archive-tag-answers-and-convergence-audit.md), decision 2; [DEC-064](DEC-064-retired-branches-kept-and-backup-commits-skip-checks.md), decision 1).

## Decision

1. **The questions, the answers, and the message are preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 34, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest. The questions and answers were taken by script from the builder's question entry of 02:49 UTC and its answer entry of 04:31 UTC in the session record, and the message from its user turn of 07:04 UTC, byte for byte. The 5-hour limit stopped the session at 04:32 UTC, 32 seconds after the answers arrived; they were pushed to this change's temporary backup at 07:13 UTC, with the message, after the session resumed at 07:02 UTC ([DEC-057](DEC-057-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md), decision 4).
2. **Stage 1 exits.** The owner's explicit approval is recorded: X8 is PASS, and with it X1; the completion certificate's "Approved to proceed" is filled; the stage is complete (constitution Rule 213; master execution constitution §143).
   - **What the approval authorizes:** the planning of Stage 2, DATA FOUNDATION, and nothing more. Stage 2's implementation begins only when the owner has approved its plan, as Stage 1's did ([DEC-038](DEC-038-stage-1-plan-approved.md); constitution Rules 134 to 136); its features stay PROPOSED until then ([DEC-040](DEC-040-later-stage-features-proposed.md)).
   - **The order:** the mapping of owner decisions 29 comes first ([DEC-062](DEC-062-preserve-and-map-universal-opportunity-directive.md), decision 3), then the workflow's change of DEC-064 (decision 2), each as its own change; then Stage 2's entry gate and plan. The mapping thus comes one change later than DEC-062's decision 3 put it, "the next change", and still where that decision places it, before Stage 2's plan.
3. **The daily figure of about 1.2% net is a measurement only**, as the owner chose and wrote: "an aspirational performance benchmark, not a mandatory daily target, trading quota, minimum opportunity threshold, or instruction to increase risk". It "must not directly determine trade frequency, position sizing, risk limits, execution decisions, or whether scanning continues", and PLT-008, TNP-014, and every existing risk, capital, execution, security, and AI-authority constraint stay as they are.
   - **Applied by the mapping, the next change:** each of the owner's seven points and conditions is mapped to the requirement that already states it, and a requirement is refined or drafted only where none does, under [owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md)'s rules and Rule 0 ([DEC-048](DEC-048-preserve-and-apply-rule-0.md)), as the message itself asks.
4. **Of adaptive execution (EXE-011), the maker-versus-taker selection alone is approved**, for the stage that builds execution. The owner's conditions: it "must evaluate the best genuinely executable, risk-adjusted net outcome, accounting for trading fees, slippage, liquidity, fill probability, latency, opportunity expiration, adverse selection, and execution reliability"; "Do not automatically prefer maker orders simply because their fees may be lower, or taker orders simply because they execute faster"; and its integration "through the existing deterministic Execution Engine without creating duplicate logic, weakening risk or capital controls, or introducing architectural drift".
   - **What stays FUTURE:** "advanced order splitting and adaptive pricing", under EXE-011, each subject to its stage's planning, approval, implementation, and verification.
   - **Nothing is built for it now:** "This approval does not authorize immediate execution-system implementation during Stage 2 Data Foundation."
   - **Applied by the mapping too:** EXE-011's text and class change there, under the same drafting rules. Until then EXE-011 reads as it did, and this record holds the approval.
5. **The certificate's backup branch is retired and kept with the twelve.** It was reconciled with `3b7a41d` by owner decisions 10's eight checks, its last commit is kept under `claude/archive/unverified-backup-x6`, and its one deletion attempt was refused with HTTP 403; owner decisions 22's ten checks passed ([Stage 1 exit verification](../traceability/stage-01-exit-verification.md)).
   - **The owner's approval** of 2026-11-10 is the explicit, recorded approval to carry it past Stage 1 (DEC-044, decision 2). It is kept until the owner removes it, and no further deletion is tried from this environment unless the owner asks (DEC-064, decision 1; [DEC-054](DEC-054-configuration-size-limit-and-retired-backup-branches.md), decision 4).
   - **Its status is written as the owner wrote it:** RETIRED — DELETION BLOCKED — NON-AUTHORITATIVE — SAFE TO DELETE WHEN PERMITTED. These are the four statements of [owner decisions 22](../handoffs/owner-decisions-22-configuration-limit-and-branch-retirement.md)'s final rule, and the same state as DEC-054's RETIRED — DELETION BLOCKED — SAFE TO DELETE WHEN PERMITTED, whose branches have no authority (the [project state](../project-state.md)'s "Branches"). From now on the current documents write the status this way for all thirteen branches, so that one state has one label; dated records keep the label they were written with.
   - **A later temporary backup branch** whose deletion this environment refuses is retired and kept the same way, after the same checks, and named in the project state's "Branches" and in its change's report, without a new question to the owner: the retirement policy the owner approved already gives the answer (the owner's words above).
6. **Nothing else changes now.** No requirement, rule, gate, value, or code changes with this record.

## Alternatives considered

- **Record the exit with the mapping, in one change**, as the project state's "Next approved step" had the mapping carry the backup's reconciliation. Not chosen: the owner's approval closes Stage 1 and is recorded at once, with its evidence; the mapping changes requirements and is reviewed on its own (constitution Rules 66, 161, and 162); and two of the answers decide questions of the mapping, so they are recorded first.
- **Apply the two answers to the requirements in this change.** Not chosen: mapping the owner's points to the requirements that already state them, and refining or drafting the rest, is the mapping's work, under owner decisions 9's rules for a batch of requirements; a part of it done here would split that review.
- **Read the approval as authorizing Stage 2's implementation.** Not chosen: the question asked for planning only, and an approval to plan is not an approval of a plan (constitution Rules 135 and 136; the quality directive's §12).
- **Keep two labels for a retired branch, the owner's for the last and DEC-054's for the twelve.** Not chosen: one state would carry two names (constitution Rule 59; master execution constitution §35).
- **Ask the owner again when a later backup branch's deletion is refused.** Not chosen: the owner asked not to be asked again for a decision the approved retirement policy already gives.
