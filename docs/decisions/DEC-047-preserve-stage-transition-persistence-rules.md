## Context

Later on 2026-10-14, while the change preserving [owner decisions 14](../handoffs/owner-decisions-14-company-grade-order-and-traceability.md) ([DEC-046](DEC-046-preserve-company-grade-order-and-traceability-rules.md)) was in its third gate, the owner sent rules for the persistence of a stage's work before any stage transition ([owner decisions 15](../handoffs/owner-decisions-15-stage-transition-persistence-and-commit-integrity.md)), marked mandatory, permanent, project-wide, and a stage-transition blocker. Every material item is classified and resolved deliberately, without committing everything blindly. A draft is a lifecycle status, not a repository state: an important draft may and often should be committed when repository policy permits, explicitly marked DRAFT, NOT APPROVED, NOT AUTHORITATIVE, NOT VERIFIED, NOT PRODUCTION-READY, and committing it promotes nothing (its §1). Every material draft gets an explicit disposition (its §7) and creates no parallel authority (its §8). The existing commit and checkpoint policy is followed, and where commits wait for a gate, an authorized candidate mechanism is used if one exists; if none exists and substantial work is at risk, the governance gap is identified and the owner's decision requested (its §10, §11). Gate 3 reviews a known repository state (its §12), and its findings and corrections are persisted (its §13, §14). Before the next stage, a final commit-completeness audit, a draft audit, a recovery test, the transition gate's conditions, and a final report in items A to V are made (its §23, §24, §27, §28, §30).

The repository's state was inventoried on 2026-10-14 (its §2): branch `claude/code-builder-constitution-rrewgf` at `bec1680`, equal to the remote; no staged change, stash, tag, or other local branch beyond `main`; the unstaged and untracked files are this change; the ignored files are the test, type, and lint caches and the virtual environment, rebuilt from the lockfile. The builder's session workspace was classified by DEC-046 (decision 5); it holds two unverified drafts of later steps, of which this record decides the disposition.

## Decision

1. **It is preserved word for word now**, as owner decisions 15, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, for the reason [DEC-042](DEC-042-preserve-anti-drift-rules-and-backup-instructions.md) gives (master execution constitution §01, §90; its own §26).
2. **It applies from 2026-10-14 to all work**, with the five builder texts and owner decisions 14; where they differ, the stricter applies (DEC-033). It is applied through the existing mechanisms, not new ones (its §10): DEC-032's checkpoint commit after the three gates, which it does not bypass; owner decisions 10's lifecycle for temporary backups; the verification records for Gate 3's findings; the [project state](../project-state.md)'s continuation contract for work not yet committed; and the [stage record](../traceability/stage-01-foundation.md)'s exit checklist. Whether it joins the builder texts that `CLAUDE.md` loads is decided with owner decisions 9's adoption, with owner decisions 10, 13, and 14 (DEC-046, decision 2).
3. **The workspace's material items are committed**, each exact and each labelled with its status, in a place that no canonical record treats as a source and that the documentation checker accepts without defining any requirement or decision (its §1, §8; master execution constitution §10: "Drafts may exist only when explicitly isolated and clearly labelled"):
   - in this change, the two unverified drafts of DEC-046's decision 5, with the scripts that hold their drafted text, as drafts in [`docs/drafts/`](../drafts/README.md), each file in a text block with the sha256 of its original: DRAFT, NOT APPROVED, NOT AUTHORITATIVE, NOT VERIFIED, NOT PRODUCTION-READY. The adoption draft's copy of owner decisions 9 keeps only its own banner, since its body is owner decisions 9's text, already preserved; two scripts show their one local path redacted (plan section 9, item 4). Their disposition (its §7), kept in the [drafts index](../drafts/README.md), is COMMITTED AS DRAFT until their steps run: the adoption of owner decisions 9 and the audit of owner decisions 8 compare them with the canonical records, map and merge what remains valid under the rules then in force, preserve the provenance, and mark each draft superseded by the record that replaces it (its §8);
   - in this change, check 7's and check 11's mutation lists and scripts, as evidence, exactly as they ran, in [Stage 1 mutation checks as run](../traceability/stage-01-mutation-checks.md); check 7 becomes maintained tooling with checkpoint C's work, as DEC-045 (decision 6) plans;
   - in the next change, the reports of earlier Gate 3 runs whose findings the records only summarize, where the builder's environment still holds them, as evidence (its §13); where none is held, the summary stays and the gap is recorded.
4. **Gate 3's findings are recorded in the repository** from this change on (its §13, §14): each run's findings, with an identity, a classification, a severity, the provenance of a blocking one, the evidence, the fix, and the status, in the change's verification record (the [traceability README](../traceability/README.md)'s format, which asks one record per checkpoint commit), for documentation changes too, not only in the commit message. This change's are in the [verification record of owner decisions 14 and 15](../traceability/owner-decisions-14-15-verification.md). Each Gate 3 run reviews a review copy identified by its base commit and the hash of its change, which its record states (its §12).
5. **Its stage-transition audit is part of Stage 1's exit**: its §23 commit-completeness audit, §24 draft audit, §27 recovery test, and §30 final report in items A to V, with §28's conditions checked, made after the stage's last checkpoint commit is pushed and before the owner's approval to proceed. The stage record's exit checklist keeps it as X10, and the [Stage 1 plan](../roadmap/stage-01-foundation-plan.md)'s "Later changes" line records it. It is the owner's addition, not the builder's.
6. **Unverified work during a long gate cycle** (its §10, §11): commits on the designated branch wait for the three gates (DEC-032), and the only candidate mechanism, owner decisions 10's temporary backup branch, was authorized for checkpoint B's work, and the only retirement the owner has decided for such a branch, for checkpoint B's backup alone ([DEC-043](DEC-043-retire-checkpoint-b-backup-under-an-archive-tag.md)), needs an archive tag that this environment cannot create ([DEC-044](DEC-044-preserve-archive-tag-answers-and-convergence-audit.md)). This is a governance gap for the next long gate cycle, checkpoint C's: it is put to the owner as [OQ-31](../open-questions/register.md), before checkpoint C starts. Until it is answered, a change in its gates stays uncommitted, as DEC-032 requires; when a session ends with one, the builder tells the owner why and how it is recovered (its §26), and the next session inspects it before anything else (master execution constitution §07).
7. **The owner's messages** (its §2, §29: unpreserved owner decisions block a transition) were compared with the repository on 2026-10-14: every message the owner typed or sent while the builder worked, the long ones word for word, and every answer the owner gave to the builder's questions. The rule texts are under `docs/builder/`; the handoffs, corrections, answers, and directives under `docs/handoffs/` (owner decisions 1 to 15, the three handoff parts, owner correction 1), with the covering lines of Part 2 and owner correction 1 in their banners; the integrity request of 2026-10-01 in the [integrity verification](../traceability/integrity-verification-2026-10-01.md), word for word; the note sent with it in the [quality audit](../traceability/quality-audit-2026-10-06.md)'s observation; the requests of 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-05 in the appendices of their audit records. Each answer to the builder's questions, with its question and options, is in the owner decisions records; the one round the owner dismissed, which has no answers, is noted in owner decisions 3. Six short messages that carry an approval to start work, an instruction, or a question were kept nowhere: five sent from 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-13, and the closing line of the message of 2026-10-01 that brought the master execution constitution and the directive, which the quality audit's account of that message leaves out. They are preserved word for word now, as [owner decisions 16](../handoffs/owner-decisions-16-short-instructions-approvals-and-questions.md), without the image of an email that came with one of them, which can hold personal details. The rest are procedural (requests to show the questions, "Continue", "Try again", notices of usage limits); owner decisions 11 and 12 keep two of them with the exchanges they belong to, and the others are not kept, nor are the prompts and notices the client sends by itself (its resume prompt, notices of restarts, the stop hook's feedback).
8. **What the comparison found is corrected** (its §2; quality directive §6): every text block of the owner records matches a message as received, and every preserved text matches the words received, except in two places; one quotation in a record had lost part of its layout.
   - [Part 1](../handoffs/part-1-core-platform-features.md)'s last line has read "the trading system" since the copy's first commit (`b850eaa`), where the owner wrote "the trading systems"; [Part 1's verification](../traceability/part-1-verification.md) had recorded the copy identical line for line. DEC-004 keeps each handoff "with its wording unchanged", so the correction is determined: the line is restored, the copy's banner records it, and its line in the preserved-text manifest changes with it. The preserved-text rule of the [documentation tools](../../tools/docs/README.md) now states this one exception: a preserved text changes only to restore the words as received, by decision, recorded in its banner.
   - [Owner decisions 8](../handoffs/owner-decisions-08-opportunity-universe-and-economics.md)'s text block began with "Continue from where you left off.", the client's automatic resume prompt of 07:50 UTC that day, not the owner's words, which reached the builder in the same turn as the message the record preserves because a usage limit fell between them.    - The [integrity verification](../traceability/integrity-verification-2026-10-01.md) quotes the owner's request of 2026-10-01 as received, word for word, but had set its item 9's list on one line and left out the blank lines between its items; both are restored, and the record's banner says so.
   - Both copies that differed were written out of the conversation by the builder, and Part 1's check compared its copy with such a copy, which carried the same slip. The preserved-text rule of the documentation tools now also states how a round trip is made: from the session record, by script, one recorded message at a time, never from a copy the builder wrote.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the drafts in the workspace until their steps run**, as DEC-046 first decided. Rejected: its §1 says an important draft may and often should be committed when repository policy permits, marked as a draft, and its §31 that no important draft may remain without a disposition; their text would otherwise depend on the container.
- **Commit the drafts beside the canonical records, or with their own decision and requirement numbers.** Rejected: they would compete with the canonical records (its §8; owner decisions 14, §28, §30), and their decision numbers are now used by other decisions.
- **Create a backup branch for every change in its gates.** Not chosen without the owner: a new branch needs the owner's permission, and this environment cannot retire one the way DEC-043 retires checkpoint B's (decision 6, OQ-31).
- **Adopt owner decisions 15 as a builder text now.** Not chosen, for the reason DEC-046 gives for owner decisions 14: the adoption step maps the new rules to the builder texts first and decides them together.
