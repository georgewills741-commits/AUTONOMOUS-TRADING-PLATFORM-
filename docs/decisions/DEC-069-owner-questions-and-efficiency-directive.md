# DEC-069 — Preserve the owner's questions of 2026-11-11 and directive of 2026-11-16 (owner decisions 35 and 36), and keep the directive in force through the existing rules

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decided by:** builder, under [DEC-004](DEC-004-preserve-original-handoffs.md) (original source material is preserved verbatim) and [DEC-047](DEC-047-preserve-stage-transition-persistence-rules.md) (decision 7: the owner's messages that carry an approval, an instruction, or a question are kept). The messages are the owner's; the directive is kept in force as owner decisions 26 is ([DEC-057](DEC-057-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md), decision 2).

## Context

On 2026-11-11, during the first Gate 3 run of the mapping of owner decisions 29 ([DEC-068](DEC-068-universal-opportunity-directive-mapped.md)), the owner asked what is remaining (11:52 UTC), how long Stage 2 will take to complete, asking the builder to continue while answering (12:00 UTC), and about upgrading to the 200-dollar plan (12:04 UTC). The client delivered each as a user turn of its own, of origin human. The builder answered each from the repository (owner decisions 35's banner). Two statements of the answers were not right, and the builder corrected them to the owner on 2026-11-16: each retired backup's work is in the commits of the designated branch, `claude/code-builder-constitution-rrewgf`, and in its archive branch, not "in the main branch", which holds only the repository's initial commit, `7172a5c`; and the client's notices of the seventeen earlier Gate 3 runs whose totals the session record holds give 315,902 to 455,716 tokens a run, wider than the answer's "roughly 370,000 to 410,000". The weekly usage limit then stopped the session from 12:11 UTC until the owner's "Try again" of 2026-11-16, 18:21 UTC.

On 2026-11-16, at 18:34 UTC, while the builder completed the mapping, the owner sent a permanent directive: substantially more efficient development, with lower token consumption and unchanged company-grade quality, through the existing rules, without duplicating them; and a concise report, A to H. The client delivered it as a message of origin human while the builder worked. The builder gave the report the same evening; it is recorded, with its evidence, in the [workflow and efficiency record](../traceability/workflow-and-efficiency-2026-11-16.md).

## Decision

1. **The messages are preserved word for word**, as [owner decisions 35](../handoffs/owner-decisions-35-questions-on-what-remains-stage-2-and-the-plan.md) (the three questions, each under its own dated heading, byte for byte, including the spaces that end four of their lines, so `git diff --check` reports those lines) and [owner decisions 36](../handoffs/owner-decisions-36-faster-development-and-lower-token-consumption.md) (the directive), HISTORICAL sources listed in the preserved-text manifest. They were taken by script from the session record on 2026-11-16. They are preserved with this change, the first whose last Gate 3 run reviews a copy made after them: the mapping's only run reviewed a copy made before them ([traceability README](../traceability/README.md), Scope).
2. **The questions change nothing.** The builder answered them; the second also asks the builder to continue while answering, which the order of work already provides ([DEC-048](DEC-048-preserve-and-apply-rule-0.md), decision 5). The choice of plan is the owner's.
3. **The directive is in force from 2026-11-16, for all work, through the existing mechanisms**, as owner decisions 26 is (DEC-057, decision 2), and adds no rule set, tool, or workflow of its own (its §2 and §11; [owner decisions 17](../handoffs/owner-decisions-17-reuse-reinforce-and-enhance-existing-controls.md), Rule 0). The [owner rule sets reconciliation](../traceability/owner-rule-sets-reconciliation.md) maps each of its points to the builder texts and mechanisms that already carry it; `CLAUDE.md` names it among the owner's rule sets in force. The three gates, the backups, the secret test, and every requirement and decision stay as they are.
4. **Its three gaps are closed by reinforcing existing practice:**
   - **The model, the tools, and the context match the task** (its §3 and §4): mechanical work, such as searching, counting, comparing, and extracting, is done by script; a low-risk read-only check may go to a smaller model; independent review (Gate 3) and work on financial correctness, security, risk, and architecture stay on the strongest model; a session loads the project state first and then only the files its task needs, by targeted reads.
   - **A scripted scan for stale statements before each Gate 3 copy** (its §5): every statement of each fact the change changes, its counts in words and in figures, phrases such as "the next change", and dates, is listed by script and read. This strengthens DEC-057's decision 6, whose read-back missed such statements: they were a frequent cause of failed review runs (the workflow and efficiency record).
   - **The cost of each change is measured** (its §8 and §12): each change's record states its Gate 3 runs with the tokens the client reports for each, its blocking findings per run, and the main session's calls and average context per call from the session record's usage fields; a figure that is not available is said to be unavailable.

## Alternatives considered

- **A separate efficiency system, rule set, or tool.** Not chosen: the directive forbids it (its §2 and §11), and the existing controls carry its points.
- **Lighter reviews to save tokens.** Not chosen: the directive keeps the three gates, and a lighter review stays the owner's to decide ([DEC-056](DEC-056-preserve-owner-question-of-2026-10-29.md)).
- **Loading the builder texts on demand instead of in full** (about 174 KB on every call, an estimated tenth of an average call's context). Not chosen: they load in full by the owner's rule ([DEC-033](DEC-033-adopt-master-execution-constitution.md); [DEC-051](DEC-051-adopt-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md)), and the larger part of each call is the conversation itself.
- **Treat the questions as procedural, or keep each message apart.** Not chosen: each asks a question or gives an instruction (DEC-047, decision 7); messages that arrive during one change are kept with it, as before ([DEC-060](DEC-060-preserve-owner-questions-of-2026-11-03.md)).

## Consequences

- Owner decisions 35 and 36 are listed in the preserved-text manifest and the documentation index; the owner rule sets reconciliation maps owner decisions 36; `CLAUDE.md` names it.
- No requirement, rule, gate, or code changes. Nothing here authorizes any stage's implementation: Stage 2's planning is authorized and its implementation is not ([DEC-067](DEC-067-stage-1-exit-and-owner-answers-of-2026-11-10.md), decision 2).
