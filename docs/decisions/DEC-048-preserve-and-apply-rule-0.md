- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Later changes:** On 2026-10-20 [DEC-051](DEC-051-adopt-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md) decided what decision 2 left to owner decisions 9's adoption: owner decisions 17 is not loaded as a builder text and stays in force through this record; the [owner rule sets reconciliation](../traceability/owner-rule-sets-reconciliation.md) shows where each of its rules is stated or applied. The text below is kept as written.
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Decided by:** builder, under [DEC-004](DEC-004-preserve-original-handoffs.md) (original source material is preserved verbatim) and the owner's checkpoint rule ([DEC-032](DEC-032-adopt-checkpoint-and-verification-rule.md)); the rule and the instruction are the owner's; how they apply until their adoption is decided (decisions 2 to 5) is the builder's, within what the rule itself lets the builder do without asking (its "NO UNNECESSARY OWNER INTERRUPTION"): an improvement that preserves approved intent, stays within existing authority, changes no material scope, system ownership, or risk or security policy, does not alter approved architecture materially, and is clearly authorized by existing engineering rules

## Context

On 2026-10-19, while the change that preserves owner decisions 14 to 16 was in its Gate 3 runs, the owner sent "RULE 0 — REUSE, REINFORCE & ENHANCE EXISTING CANONICAL CONTROLS" ([owner decisions 17](../handoffs/owner-decisions-17-reuse-reinforce-and-enhance-existing-controls.md)). Before any rule, mechanism, process, document, or other control of the kinds the rule lists is created "from this instruction", the canonical repository is inspected; where an equivalent or overlapping canonical mechanism exists, no other is created: the existing one is inspected, its authority verified, compared with the requirement and its real gaps identified, then reinforced, and enhanced where justified. Remembered knowledge is not authority: it is verified against the repository before it is relied on. An enhancement is evidence-based, keeps its provenance, checks the whole related chain from rule to operational behavior, and does not silently weaken an existing control. Routine engineering improvements within existing authority are made without asking the owner; the owner's decision is requested for a new requirement, a material architecture change, an authority reassignment, a material security-policy change, a risk-policy change, a scope expansion, a roadmap change requiring approval, a new production capability, or an unresolved conflict between canonical authorities.

The repository was inspected first, as the rule asks. Most of its substance is already canonical: search before creating, modifying, deleting, or renaming, by responsibility as well as by name (constitution Rules 37 to 41; master execution constitution §25, §164); memory never trusted over the repository (constitution Rules 48, 52; master execution constitution §01, §06); minimal change surface, no scope creep, and clean change boundaries (constitution Rules 66, 68; master execution constitution §124 to §126); root causes, not symptoms (constitution Rules 191 to 193; quality directive §6); duplicates reconciled into one canonical source (quality directive §3, §5); corrections that approved sources determine made and documented, and owner-level questions put to the owner (quality directive §2, §11, §16). [Owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md) states the same approach for requirements ("REINFORCE IT AND APPLY IT"; "Strengthen the existing canonical system rather than creating unnecessary parallel rules"), and [owner decisions 13](../handoffs/owner-decisions-13-foundation-verification-convergence-audit.md) for the convergence audit (§1: "Before creating ANY new artifact, search the repository"). What Rule 0 adds is its reach and its timing: it governs every mechanism and applies at once, and it asks that the root mechanism of a recurring problem be strengthened, not only its latest occurrence repaired.

At 19:49 UTC the same day the owner asked the builder to continue and not to stop until everything is done or the usage limits run out, and said the builder can ask questions when needed or necessary. Both messages are preserved together, as owner decisions 17.

## Decision

1. **Both messages are preserved word for word now**, as owner decisions 17, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, extracted by script from the session record and compared byte for byte, for the reason [DEC-042](DEC-042-preserve-anti-drift-rules-and-backup-instructions.md) gives (master execution constitution §01, §90).
2. **Rule 0 applies from 2026-10-19 to all work**, with the five builder texts and owner decisions 14 and 15; where they differ, the stricter applies (DEC-033). It is applied through the existing mechanisms, as it requires: it creates no procedure, register, checklist, or tracker. Whether it joins the builder texts that `CLAUDE.md` loads, and how each of its parts maps onto them, is decided with owner decisions 9's adoption (the [project state](../project-state.md)'s "Next approved step", item 3), together with owner decisions 10, 13, 14, and 15; that step's mapping covers it and is made under Rule 0 itself: each rule set against the existing canonical texts first, and only a real gap adopted, without a parallel text.
3. **It is applied at once to the gaps the work of 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-19 showed** (its "EXISTING RULE FIRST" and "ENHANCE THE ROOT"), in the existing record format, the [traceability README](../traceability/README.md):
   - **One definition of a verification record.** [DEC-047](DEC-047-preserve-stage-transition-persistence-rules.md) (decision 4) extended what a record holds: each Gate 3 run's findings with their identity, class, severity, the provenance of a blocking one, the evidence, the fix, and the status, for documentation changes too, and the copy each run reviewed, identified by its base commit and the hash of its change. The README, the format's canonical home, still said "each with its fix". It now states both, so the format is defined in one place.
   - **The owner's messages in every record.** Six short messages of the owner had been kept nowhere, the oldest for thirteen days, until they were found on 2026-10-14, when the owner's messages were compared with the repository (DEC-047, decision 7): the Gate 3 review of that change found two, and the builder's own comparison the other four ([owner decisions 16](../handoffs/owner-decisions-16-short-instructions-approvals-and-questions.md)). The existing record now names the owner's messages received after the last one the previous record names, up to the copy its last Gate 3 run reviews, each with where it is preserved, or that it is procedural, so that the preservation DEC-004 and the master execution constitution (§01, §90) require is checked at every checkpoint commit, not only when an audit asks.
   - Already done within DEC-047 (decision 8), for the same reason: the preserved-text rule of the [documentation tools](../../tools/docs/README.md) states how a round trip is made, after copies written out of the conversation had diverged from what the owner sent.
4. **The owner is still asked where Rule 0 says so.** OQ-31 stays with the owner: each of its options either needs the owner's permission for a backup branch beyond checkpoint B's (owner decisions 10; DEC-042, decision 3) or leaves substantial work only in the container, which owner decisions 10 does not want while DEC-032 holds commits until the gates pass, a conflict between canonical authorities that Rule 0 leaves to the owner.
5. **The instruction to continue applies through the existing order of work.** The builder works through the project state's "Next approved step" in order, each step through its three gates to its checkpoint commit, and goes on to the next without waiting for the owner. It asks the owner where a step needs the owner's decision, OQ-31 first, before checkpoint C, and does not take such a step until the owner answers; it stops where a gate or the master execution constitution's §153 requires it. The instruction authorizes nothing beyond DEC-038's Stage 1, whose end still waits for the owner's approval to proceed (X8), and it bypasses no gate (owner decisions 10: "Do NOT bypass DEC-032, DEC-033, Gate 3 … merely to commit faster"). When the usage limits stop the work, the continuation contract and the change's verification record say where it stopped.

## Alternatives considered

- **A new "reuse first" procedure, checklist, or register.** Rejected by Rule 0 itself: the search rules of the builder texts and the record format already carry it.
- **Adopt owner decisions 17 as a builder text now.** Not chosen: item 3 maps all the owner's rule sets onto the builder texts together, and Rule 0 asks that the mapping come first.
- **A committed tool that takes the round trip from the session record.** Not chosen: the session record belongs to the builder's environment, not to the project, and the machine checks cannot read it; the method is stated in the preserved-text rule, and each round trip's result is recorded in its verification record (master execution constitution §22).
- **A register of the owner's messages.** Rejected under Rule 0: each verification record already states what its change covers, and the messages' canonical homes are the preserved texts.

## Consequences

- Owner decisions 17 is listed in the preserved-text manifest and the documentation index.
- The [traceability README](../traceability/README.md)'s record format states DEC-047's decision 4 and names the owner's messages in each record.
- The project state records the rule in force, and item 3 of "Next approved step" covers owner decisions 17 and is made under it.
- No requirement, unit acceptance criterion, or code changes. Nothing here authorizes work beyond DEC-038's Stage 1.
