## Decision

1. **The owner's message is preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 42, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, taken by script from the session record.
2. **No server is approved (OQ-34 (c); D2).** Hetzner's CPX22 in Nuremberg stays a preferred candidate; no payment or purchase is authorized. Before the owner's final approval is asked, the builder confirms the total monthly price with backups, IPv4, the applicable taxes, and the essential storage; checks that the server supports Docker, PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, and Stage 2's workload; compares it with at least one lower-cost suitable alternative; reviews the provider's reliability, security, service terms, and hosting requirements; and explains whether it carries the initial market-data workload and which measurements would trigger an upgrade. This change makes that assessment from Hetzner's official pages ([Stage 2 record](../traceability/stage-02-data-foundation.md), "Hosting options for Stage 2's live work"). D2, MIG-004, TEC-011, and TEC-012 are unchanged: no infrastructure requirement is added, and nothing is bought.
3. **The exchanges (D1; OQ-34 (b)).** The five candidates stay the approved initial set (EXA-005). Their verification continues by permitted means only: an official page that failed is not fetched again, and no geographical restriction is circumvented ([DEC-073](DEC-073-stage-2-plan-approved-with-changes.md), decision 3). The statuses are VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED, RESTRICTED, and PENDING AUTHORIZATION, the owner's word from this decision on for the status that DEC-074's records call PENDING OWNER APPROVAL. No live trading and no authenticated exchange activity is authorized in Stage 2 (DEC-073, decision 5). Where an official document cannot be read, the owner is given its official link and the information needed.
4. **Local law (D1).** The operator's residence stays São Tomé and Príncipe (DEC-074, decision 2). The activity is taken as neither lawful nor unlawful because no specific framework has been found; authoritative sources are researched further, and the uncertainty is reported. Where a professional legal interpretation is required, it is recorded as a separate COMPLIANCE PREREQUISITE, not replaced by a legal conclusion of the builder's. Hosting in Germany does not by itself establish any regulatory authorization.
5. **Future company membership (OQ-35).** The owner decided that the platform is to become capable, in the future, of supporting approved members of a company-controlled system, an intentional evolution of the single-operator design. It is recorded as PLT-030, FUTURE, in the platform overview, in the owner's terms, among them: no automatic admission or activation of trading; each user's credentials, financial records, balances, positions, risk limits, and permissions kept separate; no pooled customer capital, custody, management of third-party funds, or trading for others without the necessary authorization and independently verified safeguards; and, before any company or third-party trading service is activated, its licensing, regulatory, privacy, consumer-protection, and compliance requirements identified. It needs dedicated product, security, compliance, architecture, and implementation decisions before construction, and it extends the established platform rather than replacing verified infrastructure. PLT-010, DEC-006, and the single-operator design govern the presently approved scope; nothing is built, and no stage holds it (ARCH-029). Custody stays FUTURE and a separate domain (CUS-001 to CUS-005; LED-008): the membership the owner describes holds no custody, and approving either approves neither.
6. **Usage.** Charges beyond the weekly usage limit are not assumed authorized; material work is preserved in the repository and on the backup branch, and resumed from the verified state. This restates what [DEC-069](DEC-069-owner-questions-and-efficiency-directive.md) and [DEC-057](DEC-057-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md) (decision 4) already provide, and adds no rule.

## Alternatives considered

- **Building member accounts now:** rejected by the owner (owner decisions 42, point 4, its implementation boundary).
- **Rewording PLT-010 to allow members:** not taken. The owner keeps the single-operator design for the presently approved scope, so PLT-010 stays as it is and PLT-030 states the future capability.
- **Recording membership under SYS-32 (CUS-001):** not taken. CUS-001 is a custody model, which the owner's decision excludes without its own authorization; a requirement of its own keeps the two apart.
- **A new system or owner for membership now:** not taken. A capability in no stage gets no system or component until its own decisions (constitution Rule 85; ARCH-029).
- **Asking the owner to approve a server now:** not taken. The owner asked for the assessment first, and Hetzner showed its CPX22 as unavailable to order ([Stage 2 record](../traceability/stage-02-data-foundation.md)).

## Consequences

PLT-030 is added, FUTURE; no other requirement changes. OQ-35 is resolved; OQ-34 stays open for the server's approval, the three venues' terms, and the law of São Tomé and Príncipe. The Stage 2 record holds the server's assessment and the further reading; the roadmap names PLT-030 among the items in no stage; DEC-006 notes the owner's decision.
