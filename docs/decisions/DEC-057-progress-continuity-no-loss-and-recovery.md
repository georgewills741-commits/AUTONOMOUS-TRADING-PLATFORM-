# DEC-057 — The owner's directive of 2026-11-02 on progress, long-term continuity, no loss of work, and recovery: preserved, mapped, and applied through the existing mechanisms (owner decisions 26)

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Decided by:** project owner (the directive, [owner decisions 26](../handoffs/owner-decisions-26-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md): mandatory and permanent, for all stages and all future sessions); the builder's: the mapping of its rules to the existing mechanisms, made under the owner's Rule 0 ([owner decisions 17](../handoffs/owner-decisions-17-reuse-reinforce-and-enhance-existing-controls.md)), and the strengthened controls below, which the directive's Rules 3, 4, 17, 27, and 31 ask for, with Rules 5, 10, and 11 for decision 4, and which lower no control; the extension of the owner's answer to OQ-31 (decision 4) is the builder's reading of them, which the owner can correct.

## Context

On 2026-11-02 at 15:43 UTC, after the session resumed from a weekly usage limit that had stopped it from 2026-10-29, the owner sent a directive marked mandatory, permanent, and project-wide. A full working month had passed with the project in Stage 1. The owner asks that the process stay rigorous and also converge: no stage open for long without an evidenced cause; progress measured; process defects fixed without weakening a quality control; finite exit criteria; no future work moved into the current stage; no material work or knowledge lost to a session's end, a container's loss, or a long pause; any loss investigated, recovered where possible, and its mechanism fixed; and a project that a future session can resume, from the repository alone, at the exact state where it stopped. It asks for an immediate status report, A to W, while the builder continues the authorized work.

The directive arrived after a loss of the kind it describes: the change reconciling checkpoint D's temporary backup existed only in the session's container when a weekly usage limit stopped the session on 2026-10-29, and the container was reset before the session resumed; the change was rebuilt from the session's record of its edits and committed as `c2a6786` ([checkpoint D backup verification](../traceability/stage-01-checkpoint-d-backup-verification.md), "Failures and fixes").

Most of the directive's rules are already stated in the builder texts or applied by existing mechanisms; the [owner rule sets reconciliation](../traceability/owner-rule-sets-reconciliation.md) maps each. Six gaps remain, closed below: no measure of a stage's convergence, or of why it stays open (its Rules 1, 3, 9, 22, 24, 33, 34); a backup made only once a gate cycle counts as long (Rules 5, 10, 11, 27); exit criteria not stated to be fixed (Rules 17, 18); a process defect that makes verification repeat, statements written from intent rather than read back and results restated across records (Rules 4, 20, 21); a session's start that does not name the temporary backups (Rules 7, 8, 31); and two verification procedures, the round trip of a preserved text and the making of a Gate 3 copy, held only in the builder's workspace (Rule 27, and the master execution constitution §22).

## Decision

1. **The directive is preserved word for word**, as [owner decisions 26](../handoffs/owner-decisions-26-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md), a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest. It was taken by script from its user turn in the session record and kept byte for byte, and its round trip is exact.
2. **It is in force from 2026-11-02, for all work, through the existing mechanisms**, as owner decisions 14, 15, and 17 are ([DEC-051](DEC-051-adopt-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md), decision 3) and owner decisions 22's two rule sets are ([DEC-054](DEC-054-configuration-size-limit-and-retired-backup-branches.md), decision 5). It is not loaded as a seventh builder text: each of its rules is stated in a builder text or applied by a mechanism, and the reconciliation's section for it says where, so loading it would state them twice (Rule 0). `CLAUDE.md` names it among the rule sets that stay in force through their decision records.
3. **A stage's convergence is measured.** The [project state](../project-state.md)'s continuation contract gains a "Stage convergence" row: the directive's classification (STRONGLY CONVERGING, CONVERGING, SLOWLY CONVERGING, STALLED, REGRESSING, or NON-CONVERGENT, its Rule 3), with its evidence (the units done and remaining, the exit items done and remaining, the open blocking findings, the independent review runs of the recent changes, check 7's last result, and any loss of work) and why the stage is still open, in the classes of its Rule 1, updated by each change. When the row reads STALLED, REGRESSING, or NON-CONVERGENT, a root-cause and process review is made before the next gate cycle, besides the triggers of [DEC-045](DEC-045-apply-foundation-convergence-audit.md)'s decision 7.
4. **Material work is backed up at once.** [DEC-052](DEC-052-apply-owner-answers-on-order-of-work-and-backups.md)'s decision 3 is strengthened: a change's material uncommitted work is pushed to the change's labelled temporary backup branch as soon as it exists, again before each Gate 3 launch and before any wait or end of a turn that leaves it uncommitted, and is reconciled with the change's commit as before, not only once its gate cycle counts as long. A session can end without warning, as the usage limit of 2026-10-29 showed.
5. **Stage 1's exit criteria are fixed.** They are the [Stage 1 plan](../roadmap/stage-01-foundation-plan.md)'s section 9 and the [stage record](../traceability/stage-01-foundation.md)'s exit checklist, X1 to X10, with the steps the project state's "Next approved step" places before the exit; they change only by the owner's decision. A finding that proposes work beyond them is recorded as later work in the stage it belongs to (DEC-045, decisions 2, 4, and 7), not added to Stage 1.
6. **Verification converges without being weakened.** Before each Gate 3 launch, every statement the change adds to its record and to the documents it touches is read back against its evidence, since many failed runs of the recent changes found statements written from intent or memory rather than read back against their evidence (the "Failures and fixes" of the records of checkpoints C and D, of DEC-054's change, and of the reconciliation of checkpoint D's backup). A record states each result once and cites other records rather than restating them. A step that closes earlier work, such as a temporary backup's reconciliation or the preservation of a message that changes nothing, is carried by the next change instead of a change of its own. Evidence a change does not touch is reused, not rerun (DEC-045, decision 5). No gate is lowered: a lighter review for changes that touch no code, requirement, or decision, which the builder offered on 2026-10-29, stays the owner's to decide ([DEC-056](DEC-056-preserve-owner-question-of-2026-10-29.md)).
7. **A long pause can be resumed from the repository.** `CLAUDE.md`'s "Where to start" adds the inspection of the remote branches, since an active temporary backup holds work not yet committed (the project state's "Branches"). The round trip of a preserved text is written down exactly in the [documentation tools](../../tools/docs/README.md)' rule for preserved texts, and the making of a Gate 3 copy in the [traceability README](../traceability/README.md), so that a fresh session repeats each without the builder's workspace.
8. **The status report and the loss audit** the directive asks for are recorded in the [verification record](../traceability/owner-decisions-26-verification.md) of the change that preserves it, so that they do not exist only in the session (its Rule 15).

## Alternatives considered

- **Load the directive as a seventh builder text.** Not chosen: its rules are already stated or applied (decision 2), as owner decisions 14, 15, and 17's were when DEC-051 kept them in force through their records, and owner decisions 22's when DEC-054 did; a seventh text would state them a second time (Rule 0).
- **Commit scripts for the round trip and the Gate 3 copy under `tools/`.** Not chosen now: the round trip reads the client's session record, whose format belongs to the client, not to the project, and what must be repeatable is the procedure, which decision 7 writes down; a script can be added when a stage's plan needs such tooling.
- **Keep DEC-052's trigger, a backup once a gate cycle counts as long.** Not chosen: on 2026-10-29 the session ended without warning while the change's first Gate 3 run was still running, so neither of the trigger's conditions, a failed first run or a session known to be ending, was seen in time, and the work was lost.
- **Lower a gate to go faster.** Not the builder's to decide, and the directive forbids it (its Rule 19); decision 6 removes repetition instead.

## Consequences

- Owner decisions 26 is listed in the preserved-text manifest and the documentation index; the owner rule sets reconciliation gains its section; `CLAUDE.md` names it, and its "Where to start" names the remote branches.
- The project state gains the "Stage convergence" row; the stage record's exit checklist states that its items change only by the owner's decision.
- DEC-052 gains a "Later changes" line pointing to decision 4.
- The tools README and the traceability README state the two procedures of decision 7, and the traceability README the read-back of decision 6.
- No requirement, system rule, gate, or code changes. Nothing here authorizes work beyond [DEC-038](DEC-038-stage-1-plan-approved.md)'s Stage 1.
