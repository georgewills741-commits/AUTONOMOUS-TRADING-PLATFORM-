Stage 2's checkpoint A was committed as `ec4de57` after its three gates, and the machine checks passed on it ([run 44551519578](https://github.com/georgewills741-commits/AUTONOMOUS-TRADING-PLATFORM-/actions/runs/44551519578)). After the owner's questions on (c), on exchanges that serve the United States, and on a server's cost and control, the builder put five questions to the owner (17:26 and 19:00 UTC); the owner answered them at 19:26 UTC.

## Decision

1. **The owner's messages are preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 41, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, with the builder's five questions as shown; the owner's "Continue" of 14:46 UTC is procedural ([DEC-047](DEC-047-preserve-stage-transition-persistence-rules.md), decision 7).
2. **The operator (OQ-34 (a)).** The operator is an individual resident in São Tomé and Príncipe; no company is assumed, and no other residence. For the current development plan the platform is a private, single-operator system that trades the owner's own authorized capital through the owner's own eligible exchange accounts, as PLT-010 states ([DEC-006](DEC-006-single-operator-and-trading-ledger.md)); it is still engineered to company-grade standards and operates independently of the builder (the six builder texts). The answer's unfinished clause on others connecting theirs would change PLT-010, which excludes multi-user account functions; it is not applied, and goes to the owner as OQ-35.
3. **The exchanges (OQ-34 (b); the owner's answers 4 and 5).** The five candidates, Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Bybit, and KuCoin, stay as they are (EXA-005; [DEC-008](DEC-008-venues-and-trading-universe.md)): none is removed or replaced. Option B was applied on 2026-11-19, each venue's official pages fetched once, and the result for an individual resident of São Tomé and Príncipe is in the [Stage 2 record](../traceability/stage-02-data-foundation.md), "Venue verification for the operator's country": OKX's terms permit its public market data for the operator's own trading, its endpoint not yet checked; KuCoin RESTRICTED for Stage 2, its terms requiring registration before its quotations; Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase NOT VERIFIED, their terms unread again; the law of São Tomé and Príncipe NOT VERIFIED. A venue stays disabled for public market data until D1's verification passes for it: its terms and the applicable law permit credential-free, read-only access, and its public endpoint answers from the approved environment without any workaround ([DEC-073](DEC-073-stage-2-plan-approved-with-changes.md), decision 3). The owner's permission to research other reputable exchanges is used as research only: Kraken and Bitget were read, both NOT VERIFIED; a new venue enters only through the existing assessment, as EXA-006 provides, and the owner's approval, and none is added here. Public access is never taken as permission to trade (DEC-073, decision 3).
4. **The environment of Stage 2's live work (OQ-34 (c); D2).** An environment the owner provides, as D2 allows (MIG-004): a rented server in Germany, the owner's preference for Stage 2's development environment. Tokyo is not suitable for Stage 2, since OKX names Japan among its Restricted Locations. The production location is chosen later from measured latency, permitted access, reliability, security, and cost, as D2 already provides (production's baselines come from the production host, OPERATIONALIZATION). US$10 to 25 a month is a provisional research ceiling, not an approval to spend: the builder's comparison is in the Stage 2 record, "Hosting options for Stage 2's live work", and no server is bought or provisioned before the owner approves a specific offer (D2; DEC-073, decision 6). The architecture is not reduced to fit the ceiling: where the workload needs more, the record says so.
5. **The owner's permanent conditions are existing rules, which apply unchanged:** Stage 2 public-market-data only and read-only, with no private account access, trading credentials, or live orders (DEC-073, decisions 5 and 6); the deterministic architecture, the risk controls, the Global Capital Authority, financial correctness, the AI authority restrictions, security, recovery, and auditability (the requirements that state them); the three gates and durable checkpoints ([DEC-032](DEC-032-adopt-checkpoint-and-verification-rule.md), [DEC-033](DEC-033-adopt-master-execution-constitution.md), [DEC-045](DEC-045-apply-foundation-convergence-audit.md)); existing mechanisms reused, not duplicated ([DEC-048](DEC-048-preserve-and-apply-rule-0.md)); the work kept efficient ([DEC-069](DEC-069-owner-questions-and-efficiency-directive.md)). No requirement is added, removed, or changed.
6. **OQ-34 is narrowed, not closed.** Answered: (a), and (c) in principle. Open: the terms of Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase, which the owner is asked to provide; a confirmation that the law of São Tomé and Príncipe permits the operator's access, for which no official source was found; and the owner's approval of a specific server offer, without which no venue's endpoint is checked.

## Alternatives considered

- **A server in Tokyo** for latency to Asian venues: not for Stage 2, since OKX restricts Japan; latency decides production's location, measured later.
- **Checking venues from this environment:** not permitted; it is in the United States, which the venues' terms read restrict in part or in whole.
- **Reading the pages that stayed unreadable by other routes** (archived copies, other fetchers): refused by this session's permission controls on 2026-11-17 and not attempted another way; the owner asked not to repeat failed attempts.
- **Applying the clause on others' accounts:** it would change PLT-010 without the owner's decision.
- **Adding Kraken or another venue now:** not before the assessment and the owner's approval.
