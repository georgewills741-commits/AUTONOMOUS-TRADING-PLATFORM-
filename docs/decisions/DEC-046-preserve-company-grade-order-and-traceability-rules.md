## Context

On 2026-10-14, after the Foundation convergence audit was committed (`bec1680`) and reported, the owner sent rules for the order, traceability, and auditability of the project ([owner decisions 14](../handoffs/owner-decisions-14-company-grade-order-and-traceability.md)), marked mandatory, permanent, and project-wide. Everything material has a purpose, an owner, a canonical location, a status, a history, a dependency context, a verification path, and a traceability path. For important functionality, traceability runs from the owner's intent through decision, requirement, architecture, implementation, configuration, test, and verification to release and operation, and back where appropriate (its §2). Its sections cover requirements, decisions, ownership, document classes, status, change control and impact, history, commits, findings, root causes, dependencies, interfaces, tests, verification, releases, environments, configuration, security, financial controls, AI decisions, operations, incidents, auditability, hidden state, shadow processes, exceptions, documentation, quality gates, technical debt, TODOs, reviews, and handoff. It asks that existing lifecycles and mechanisms be used and reinforced, never duplicated (its §7, §28, §30, §31), and that, before Foundation exits, a company-grade order audit be reported in 22 items (its §40).

Two of its rules were checked at once. No TODO, FIXME, XXX, or HACK marker is in `src/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, `contracts/`, `pyproject.toml`, or `.github/` (its §34; `git grep` on 2026-10-14). And the builder's session workspace was listed against its §27, which asks that material state be preserved through the canonical repository, configuration, storage, or operational systems, not only in memory, private scratchpads, temporary scripts, or transient container state: decision 5 classifies what it holds. The listing found, besides check 7's list of mutations and its script, which [DEC-045](DEC-045-apply-foundation-convergence-audit.md) (decision 6) commits with checkpoint C's work, and the mutations of checkpoint A's check 11, unverified drafts of two later steps that no repository record mentioned: the state the owner calls forgotten ([owner decisions 10](../handoffs/owner-decisions-10-unverified-backup-and-lifecycle.md): "There must be NO forgotten state"). They were found by this record's Gate 3 review. They were drafted between 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-12, while checkpoint B's reviews ran, and the continuation contract, which names work in progress, recorded only the repository's own state.

## Decision

1. **It is preserved word for word now**, as owner decisions 14, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, for the reason [DEC-042](DEC-042-preserve-anti-drift-rules-and-backup-instructions.md) gives: no owner instruction exists only in the session (master execution constitution §01, §90; its own §27).
2. **It applies from 2026-10-14 to all work**, with the five builder texts; where they differ, the stricter applies (DEC-033). The owner marked it mandatory and permanent, and it is applied through the existing mechanisms, not new ones (its §7, §28, §30, §31): the [requirements registry](../requirements/registry.md) and its [conventions](../requirements/README.md); the [decision log](README.md) and the decision records with their "Later changes" lines; the [system registry](../architecture/system-registry.md), the [source-of-truth map](../architecture/source-of-truth-map.md), and the [dependency map](../architecture/dependency-map.md); the [findings register](../conflicts/register.md) and the [open-question register](../open-questions/register.md); the verification records, the [stage record](../traceability/stage-01-foundation.md), and the [project state](../project-state.md) with its continuation contract and checkpoint log; GOV-024's feature lifecycle and the [status labels](../README.md). Owner decisions 9 changes nothing until its adoption, step 3 of decision 6 ([DEC-042](DEC-042-preserve-anti-drift-rules-and-backup-instructions.md), decision 2), and this record does not revisit that decision; step 2 is done under the five builder texts and these rules. Whether these rules join the builder texts that `CLAUDE.md` loads is decided with owner decisions 9's adoption, together with owner decisions 10's lifecycle and owner decisions 13's lasting rules (DEC-042, decision 7; [DEC-044](DEC-044-preserve-archive-tag-answers-and-convergence-audit.md), decision 4). That step first maps them to the builder texts and to those mechanisms, as the [convergence audit](../traceability/foundation-convergence-audit-2026-10-13.md) did for owner decisions 13 (section 9), and records each gap it finds with its handling. Until then they apply through this record, which the project state names.
3. **Its platform rules are reconciled with the requirements** after owner decisions 9 is adopted, under its drafting rules: §18 to §25 (the traceability of releases, environments, configuration, security, financial controls, AI decisions, operations, and incidents). Each is mapped to the requirements that already state it; what no requirement states is drafted under those rules, or put to the owner where it needs a decision. No requirement changes here.
4. **Its §40 report is an exit item of Stage 1**, beside the eight completion criteria of the [Stage 1 plan](../roadmap/stage-01-foundation-plan.md) (section 9): before Foundation exits, the builder reports §40's 22 items, each from a review actually performed and with its evidence ("Do not claim company-grade order without performing this review"). The stage record's exit checklist keeps it as X9, and the plan's "Later changes" line records it. It is the owner's addition, not the builder's (owner decisions 13, §8, §19). Its §31 applies to U7's matrix at checkpoint D: the matrix reinforces the registry and the [traceability README](../traceability/README.md)'s format, as the plan says, and its design states how each link of §31's chain is reached, from the matrix's columns or the registry's, without a second registry; U7's acceptance criteria are unchanged.

## Alternatives considered

- **Adopt owner decisions 14 as a sixth builder text now.** Not chosen: like owner decisions 13 (DEC-044), it asks first that existing mechanisms be used and reinforced (its §28, §30, §31), and the adoption step maps which of its rules the builder texts already state. Deciding owner decisions 9, 10, 13, and 14 together keeps the builder texts one coherent set; until then this record applies it.
- **Keep check 7's list and script in the workspace until checkpoint C**, as DEC-045 planned. Rejected: the workspace is not durable, and its §27 and owner decisions 10 both forbid material work existing only there. They are committed as evidence in this change; the maintained tooling stays with checkpoint C (DEC-045, decision 6).
- **Make the §40 audit now.** Not chosen: §40 asks for it before Foundation exits, and made now it would be out of date by then, since checkpoints C and D change the repository. It is made with checkpoint D, before the stage's own gates, which then verify its corrections; the mapping of decision 2 and the reconciliation of decision 3 find its gaps earlier.
- **Keep the drafts of steps 3 and 4 in the workspace, named in the repository, until their steps run.** First chosen here, then replaced the same day by owner decisions 15, whose §1 says an important draft may and often should be committed when repository policy permits, marked as a draft: they are committed as drafts in this change, in `docs/drafts/`, where they compete with no canonical record (DEC-047, decision 3). Committing them beside the canonical records, or with their own decision numbers, now used for other decisions, stays rejected (its §28, §30).
- **Preserve the briefs of past Gate 3 runs.** Not chosen: they hold the builder's working paths and instructions; what they establish is recorded where the audit recorded it, and the remaining gap stays recorded.
