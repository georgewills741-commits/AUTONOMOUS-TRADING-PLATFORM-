## Decision

1. **The message is preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 46, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest: taken by script from its user turn in the session record and kept byte for byte, with the spaces that end its first and last lines, so `git diff --check` reports those lines.
2. **The builder pursues those items no further.** It reads no more official pages and puts no more requests to the owner on the terms of Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase or on the law of São Tomé and Príncipe and of the server's location. They stay open under OQ-34, unconfirmed; Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase stay NOT VERIFIED and disabled.
3. **The message changes no rule.** Not to worry about the items is read as setting them aside, not as confirming them or waiving the conditions they decide: a venue is enabled for public market data only once verified ([DEC-073](DEC-073-stage-2-plan-approved-with-changes.md), decision 3), and the activity is taken as neither lawful nor unlawful until an official source, the owner's confirmation, or, where required, a professional legal interpretation settles the law ([DEC-075](DEC-075-owner-operating-decisions-and-future-membership.md), decision 4). Those are the owner's explicit decisions of 2026-11-19, which a general remark does not silently change ([owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md), Rules 9 and 27); the builder's answer said so, and the owner may decide otherwise by a new decision. The conditions matter only when a venue is to be enabled, which needs an approved server too; the work that needs no venue's data does not depend on them.
4. **The alternatives are recorded, not added.** HTX, BingX, Gate, and Bitget, and the others the owner may name, are venues the owner names as alternatives: candidates PROPOSED by the owner, for research when a venue is needed, not part of the approved initial candidate set, which stays Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Bybit, and KuCoin (EXA-005; [DEC-074](DEC-074-owner-answers-on-jurisdiction-operator-server-and-exchanges.md), decision 3). Each is added only after the compliance, security, technical, liquidity, fee, reliability, and integration assessments the owner named, D1's verification for the operator, and the owner's approval (DEC-074, decision 3); its addition is then a new adapter and its configuration, with no platform change (EXA-006). None is researched now: no venue's data is needed before a server is approved, and Bitget's two official pages tried on 2026-11-19 answered HTTP 404 ([Stage 2 record](../traceability/stage-02-data-foundation.md), "Venue verification for the operator's country").
5. **It is preserved by the change that builds U4's parts that need no venue's data**, whose record names it: the record of the change that records owner decisions 45 leaves it to the next record ([owner decisions 45 verification](../traceability/owner-decisions-45-verification.md), "Final status and remaining issues"), and this change's Gate 3 reviews a copy made after it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Read the message as the owner's confirmation of the law, or as waiving a venue's verification.** Not chosen: the owner's decisions of 2026-11-19 ask for an official source, the owner's confirmation, or a professional legal interpretation, and enable no venue unverified; a general remark is not an explicit decision to change them.
- **Add HTX, BingX, Gate, and Bitget to the candidates now, or research them now.** Not chosen: the owner named them as alternatives, not additions; the five candidates stay as the owner set them; a venue enters only through DEC-074's assessments and the owner's approval; and researching venues not yet needed would spend effort the owner asked not to spend on blocked work.
- **Ask the owner what the message meant.** Not chosen: the builder's reading keeps every existing decision and blocks no work now, and its answer of 21:16 UTC states it, so the owner can correct it without a question to answer.
