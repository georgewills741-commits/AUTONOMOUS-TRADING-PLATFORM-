## Decision

1. **The messages are preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 46, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest: each taken by script from its user turn in the session record and kept byte for byte, the first with the spaces that end its first and last lines, so `git diff --check` reports those lines.
2. **The builder pursues those items no further, nor any exchange's terms.** It reads no more official pages and puts no more requests to the owner on the terms of Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase or on the law of São Tomé and Príncipe and of the server's location, and, at the second message's word, on the terms of any exchange, the alternatives' included. The items stay open under OQ-34, unconfirmed; Binance, Bybit, and Coinbase stay NOT VERIFIED and disabled.
3. **The messages change no rule.** Not to worry about the items, and to forget about the exchanges' terms, are read as ending the builder's work on them, not as confirming them or waiving the conditions they decide: a venue's public market data is monitored only where access to it is permitted by applicable law and the venue's terms and is verified ([DEC-073](DEC-073-stage-2-plan-approved-with-changes.md), decision 3), and the activity is taken as neither lawful nor unlawful while no framework is found, a professional legal interpretation, where required, being a separate compliance prerequisite ([DEC-075](DEC-075-owner-operating-decisions-and-future-membership.md), decision 4). Those are the owner's explicit decisions of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-19, which a general remark, even repeated, does not silently change ([owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md), Rules 9 and 27); the builder's answer of 21:16 UTC said so, and the owner may decide otherwise by a new decision. The conditions matter only when a venue is to be enabled, which needs an approved server too; the work that needs no venue's data does not depend on them. The second message followed the builder's answer of 21:16 UTC and may correct it; when a server is approved and a venue is otherwise ready, the builder asks the owner once, with that approval, whether these conditions are waived by a new decision or are to be met by the owner's confirmation or a professional legal interpretation; that question is not a further pursuit of the items.
4. **The alternatives are recorded, not added.** HTX, BingX, Gate, and Bitget, and the others the owner may name, are venues the owner names as alternatives: candidates PROPOSED by the owner, for research when a venue is needed, not part of the approved initial candidate set, which stays Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Bybit, and KuCoin (EXA-005; [DEC-074](DEC-074-owner-answers-on-jurisdiction-operator-server-and-exchanges.md), decision 3). Each is added only after the compliance, security, technical, liquidity, fee, reliability, and integration assessments the owner named, D1's verification for the operator, and the owner's approval (DEC-074, decision 3); its addition is then a new adapter and its configuration, with no platform change (EXA-006). None is researched now: no venue's data is needed before a server is approved, and Bitget's two official pages tried on 2026-11-19 answered HTTP 404 ([Stage 2 record](../traceability/stage-02-data-foundation.md), "Venue verification for the operator's country").
5. **The next thing is asked of the owner, the current work continuing meanwhile.** The builder continues the work the items do not block, U4's parts that need no venue's data. In the plan's order (its section 11), what follows is the rest of checkpoint B, each venue's adapter, which waits for a verified venue and an approved server. Checkpoint C's units, U6 storage and U7 the Quantitative Engine with the Fee and Slippage Engines, need neither, but the plan puts them after checkpoint B, and the owner approved it to proceed with the authorized checkpoints "according to the established roadmap" ([owner decisions 40](../handoffs/owner-decisions-40-approval-of-stage-2-plan-with-changes.md); DEC-073, decision 6: "checkpoint by checkpoint, in the plan's order"). Building them first changes that order, which is the owner's to decide (master execution constitution §64; the quality directive's §11): the builder asks it with this change's report, recorded as OQ-36 in the [open-question register](../open-questions/register.md), recommending it, and starts no unit of checkpoint C before the answer.
6. **They are preserved by the change that builds U4's parts that need no venue's data**, whose record names them: the record of the change that records owner decisions 45 leaves the first to the next record ([owner decisions 45 verification](../traceability/owner-decisions-45-verification.md), "Final status and remaining issues"), and this change's Gate 3 reviews a copy made after both.

## Alternatives considered

- **Read the messages as the owner's confirmation of the law, or as waiving a venue's verification.** Not chosen: the owner's decisions of 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-19 enable no venue unverified and leave the law to authoritative sources or, where required, a professional legal interpretation; a general remark, even repeated, is not an explicit decision to change them, and nothing acts on the reading before a venue is to be enabled, when the owner is asked once whether the second message waives them (decision 3).
- **Add HTX, BingX, Gate, and Bitget to the candidates now, or research them now.** Not chosen: the owner named them as alternatives, not additions; the five candidates stay as the owner set them; a venue enters only through DEC-074's assessments and the owner's approval; and researching venues not yet needed would spend effort the owner asked not to spend on blocked work.
- **Ask the owner what the messages meant.** Not chosen: the builder's reading keeps every existing decision and blocks no work now; its answer of 21:16 UTC states it, and this change's report states it again, so the owner can correct it without a question to answer.
- **Read "proceed to next thing" as approving checkpoint C ahead of checkpoint B's adapters, and start it.** Not chosen: the owner approved the plan's checkpoints to be built in its order, and a change to that order is the owner's decision, which a general remark does not make; the question needs one answer, and the work it does not block goes on meanwhile.
