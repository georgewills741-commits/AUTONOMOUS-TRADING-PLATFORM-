# DEC-068 — The owner's directive of 2026-11-06 mapped to the canonical requirements, with the owner's answers of 2026-11-10 applied

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decided by:** project owner (the directive, [owner decisions 29](../handoffs/owner-decisions-29-universal-opportunity-intelligence-and-profit-optimization.md), which asks for its most valuable parts to be put where they belong; and the answers and message of 2026-11-10, [owner decisions 34](../handoffs/owner-decisions-34-approval-to-plan-stage-2-and-answers-on-the-directive.md), on its daily figure and on maker-or-taker execution, which [DEC-067](DEC-067-stage-1-exit-and-owner-answers-of-2026-11-10.md) records); the builder's: the mapping to the existing requirements, the placement and wording of the new requirements, which follow the owner's own words and the established requirements they cite, the value's class, and the readings below
- **Resolves:** [DEC-062](DEC-062-preserve-and-map-universal-opportunity-directive.md)'s decision 3 (the mapping of the directive's parts) and DEC-067's decisions 3 and 4 (the two answers, applied to the requirements by the mapping)

## Context

On 2026-11-06 the owner sent a directive in twenty sections: the platform's objective, discovery, intensity, exchanges, net profitability, allocation, small gains, strategies, AI, daily performance, execution, measurement, and capital protection (§3 to §15), and the builder's role, audit, gates, root-cause correction, change management, workflow, and report (§1, §2, §16 to §20). Its first line asks the builder to take its most valuable parts and put them where they belong, without mistakes. [DEC-062](DEC-062-preserve-and-map-universal-opportunity-directive.md) preserved it word for word and gave the mapping to a later change, the change after the one that records Stage 1's exit (DEC-067, decision 2).

On 2026-11-10 the owner answered the two questions it raised: its daily figure of about 1.2% net is a measurement only, used in reports and never as an input to scanning, sizing, risk, or execution, with PLT-008 and TNP-014 unchanged; and, of adaptive execution (EXE-011), the maker-versus-taker selection alone is approved, for the stage that builds execution, order splitting and adaptive pricing staying future capabilities under EXE-011. The owner's message of that day added the conditions of both answers and seven points the platform must continue to meet, and asked for the existing canonical requirements to be inspected first, reused, and reinforced, without duplicate rules, conflicting objectives, additional systems, or unauthorized architectural changes.

The [universal opportunity mapping](../traceability/universal-opportunity-mapping-2026-11-11.md) compared each point of the directive and each of the owner's seven points with the specifications, the registers, the roadmap, and the builder texts. Most were already canonical: from Handoff Part 1, Parts 2 and 3, DEC-014, and the additions of [DEC-058](DEC-058-opportunity-universe-and-economics.md) and [DEC-059](DEC-059-platform-traceability-rules.md). No contradiction was found. Eight statements were made by no requirement, or only in general terms, and the owner's two answers had no requirement yet.

## Decision

1. **The directive is applied through the canonical requirements,** not through its text, which stays a HISTORICAL source; so are the owner's answers and message of 2026-11-10.
2. **What is already canonical stays where it is.** The mapping record maps each of the directive's twenty sections, and each of the owner's seven points of 2026-11-10, to the requirements, system rules, decisions, and builder texts that state them; none is restated.
3. **Eight requirements are added**, each to the specification that owns its subject, each naming the requirements it extends ("In addition to …"), in the owner's words where the owner gave them:
   - OPP-023: the search order is a preference, not a blocking sequence; several tiers may be evaluated at once (§4).
   - OPP-024: adaptive discovery intensity, a function of the Opportunity Detection Engine (SYS-05), not a separate component; no repeated analysis of identical or stale conditions; more aggressive scanning never causes more trades, larger positions, higher leverage, or relaxed risk limits by itself (§5).
   - TNP-031: each cost is counted once; a gross market spread is not net profitability (§7).
   - EXE-012: the maker-versus-taker selection, with the owner's conditions (DEC-067, decision 4; §13).
   - PFC-020: account-level net return, capital-weighted, never the sum of allocations' returns (§8, §9; the owner's seventh point).
   - PFC-021: daily performance over meaningful periods and the frequency of days at each level, the owner's benchmark among them, which is for measurement only (DEC-067, decision 3; §12, §14).
   - PFC-022: results labelled by their origin; hypothetical results never presented as real; improvement by evidence, not by chasing the benchmark (§14; the owner's message).
   - STR-029, FUTURE: statistical arbitrage, and funding-rate and basis strategies, as research candidates (§10).
   None of the existing requirements is reworded, as DEC-058's decision 3 gives the reason: each keeps the source it was approved from. EXE-011 keeps its text and its class: its order splitting and adaptive pricing stay future capabilities under it, as the owner wrote, and the maker-versus-taker selection it also covered is approved as EXE-012.
4. **One value is registered** (ARCH-018): V-44, the owner's daily benchmark of about 1.2% net and the levels from 1.0% to 1.5% whose frequency PFC-021 reports, DEFAULT, a reporting reference only. It is not a DESIGN TARGET, which [DEC-020](DEC-020-value-classification.md) defines as a soft target for design and optimization: the owner chose a measurement only.
5. **No system rule is added; SR-19 is reinforced** (owner decisions 17, Rule 0): PFC-021, whose benchmark must not directly determine trade frequency, position sizing, risk limits, execution decisions, or whether scanning continues, joins SR-19's canonical requirements, beside PLT-008 and TNP-014.
6. **Each new requirement is mapped to its roadmap stage** (the roadmap's "Additions of 2026-11-11 by stage"): OPP-023 and OPP-024 to DATA FOUNDATION; TNP-031 and EXE-012 to CORE TRADING FOUNDATION; PFC-020 to PFC-022 to DIRECTIONAL TRADING, with the Performance Controller's core; STR-029 to no stage. The approval of EXE-012 authorizes no implementation in Stage 2 (DEC-067).
7. **The builder's parts** (§1, §2, §16 to §20) are mapped to the six builder texts and the owner's rule sets; DEC-032's gates keep their names and their meaning (owner decisions 9, Rules 3 and 27). One gap is closed by reinforcing an existing rule, not by a new one: `CLAUDE.md`'s "Where to start", which since [DEC-057](DEC-057-progress-continuity-no-loss-and-recovery.md)'s decision 7 has a session compare the project state with the remote's branches, adds the remote's open pull requests (§2, item 7: "Check current branches and pull requests to avoid repeating work already underway").
8. **Terminology** ([glossary](../glossary.md)): the directive's "Adaptive Opportunity Intensity Controller" is OPP-024's function of SYS-05, and its "daily objective" is V-44's benchmark, a measurement.
9. **The two questions of 2026-11-10 on the directive are recorded** in the [open-question register](../open-questions/register.md) as OQ-32 and OQ-33, with the owner's answers.
10. **The owner's requirement drafting rules were applied** ([owner decisions 9](../handoffs/owner-decisions-09-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md), as [DEC-051](DEC-051-adopt-requirement-drafting-and-anti-drift-rules.md) reads them): each drafted item was classified, its provenance in the owner's words and its owner stated, and the existing requirements it strengthens named; no existing requirement's text changes. The final reconciliation report (Rule 23) is in the mapping record. No further owner decision is needed: each new requirement states what the directive or the owner's message says, or what an established requirement it cites determines.
11. **Owner decisions 29's status banner** names this decision as the mapping instead of "the next change"; a status banner may change by decision, and the text the preserved-text manifest's line covers does not change ([DEC-047](DEC-047-preserve-stage-transition-persistence-rules.md), decision 8).
12. **Nothing is implemented.** This decision changes documentation only. Stage 2's planning is authorized and its implementation is not (DEC-067, decision 2); each new requirement is built by the stage the roadmap places it in, under that stage's approved plan ([DEC-038](DEC-038-stage-1-plan-approved.md); [DEC-040](DEC-040-later-stage-features-proposed.md)).

## Reading notes (builder)

- **The directive's audit statuses** (§2: VERIFIED EXISTING, EXISTING BUT INCOMPLETE, IMPLEMENTED BUT UNVERIFIED, MISSING, CONFLICTING, UNNECESSARY DUPLICATE, BLOCKED) describe implementation: "Implemented and supported by current evidence", "No valid implementation exists". Only Stage 1 is built, so the mapping gives each point two statuses: where its requirement stands (owner decisions 9, Rule 8: existing, refined, new, future), and its implementation in the directive's terms, which for every point beyond Stage 1's delivered parts is BLOCKED until its stage's plan is approved. A requirement is never marked complete because it is documented (§2: "A requirement must not be marked complete merely because its name appears in a document or its code exists").
- **The directive's three gates** (§16) are DEC-032's three, named and ordered differently: its architecture gate is Verification 2, its functional and quantitative gate Verification 1, and its operational readiness gate Verification 3 together with GOV-024's PAPER/SAFE TEST and CANARY states and the master execution constitution's §104 and §105. The mapping record gives the correspondence; nothing is renamed.
- **Graphify** is not available in this environment; the directive asks for it only "when available and materially useful" and says: "Do not fabricate findings if a tool or repository is inaccessible." The mapping used git, the documentation tools, and the repository's own indexes.

## Alternatives considered

- **Reword EXE-011, or replace it with two requirements.** Not chosen: the owner wrote "Keep advanced order splitting and adaptive pricing as future capabilities under EXE-011", and a rewording would change what DEC-027 approved and mix the sources (DEC-058, decision 3).
- **A component named "Adaptive Opportunity Intensity Controller".** Not chosen: market monitoring has one owner (OPP-010), the directive asks for it to be created or refined "using existing infrastructure wherever possible", and the owner's message forbids additional systems (Rule 0).
- **A system rule of its own for the daily benchmark.** Not chosen: SR-19 already governs fixed daily targets; PFC-021 joins it.
- **A requirement for each point of the directive.** Not chosen: what a requirement already states is mapped, not restated (owner decisions 9, Rules 5 and 10).
- **V-44 as a DESIGN TARGET.** Not chosen: that class steers design and optimization (DEC-020); the owner chose "Measurement only".
