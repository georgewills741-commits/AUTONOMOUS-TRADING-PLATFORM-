- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decided by:** the project owner (the approval and its changes, [owner decisions 40](../handoffs/owner-decisions-40-approval-of-stage-2-plan-with-changes.md)); the builder's: how the plan states them.

## Context

On 2026-11-17 the change that writes Stage 2's entry gate and plan was committed as `9d1104c` after its three gates, and the machine checks passed on it ([run 44137802538](https://github.com/georgewills741-commits/AUTONOMOUS-TRADING-PLATFORM-/actions/runs/44137802538)). At 18:58 UTC the owner answered: approval with changes. Stage 2's implementation waits for the owner's approval of its plan ([DEC-067](DEC-067-stage-1-exit-and-owner-answers-of-2026-11-10.md), decision 2; constitution Rules 134 to 136).

## Decision

1. **The questions and the answers are preserved word for word**, as owner decisions 40, a HISTORICAL source listed in the preserved-text manifest, taken by script from the session record, as owner decisions 34 and 38 keep theirs.
2. **The plan is approved, with changes.** The owner approves its overall technical direction and its decisions D3 to D12 as recommended, subject to the existing canonical requirements, and decides D1 and D2 as decisions 3 and 4 state. The [Stage 2 plan](../roadmap/stage-02-data-foundation-plan.md) is revised to state them wherever it relied on D1's confirmation or on the environment as observed: its banner, entry gate, section 3's rows of EXA-005 and EXA-007 (EXA-007 now started in Stage 2 and completed before any trading), U1, section 5, D1, D2, and D11's capture of recorded data in section 10, the sequence, and the risks; its other parts are unchanged. Stage 2's features become APPROVED, and DESIGNED as the plan designs them ([DEC-040](DEC-040-later-stage-features-proposed.md)).
3. **D1: the exchanges.** The five initial venues, Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Bybit, and KuCoin, stay the candidate market-data sources (EXA-005; [DEC-008](DEC-008-venues-and-trading-universe.md)), and the architecture stays open to more (EXA-006); none is assumed available. A venue's support, lawful access to its public market data, the account's eligibility, and trading authorization are kept apart. In Stage 2 a venue's public market data is monitored only where credential-free, read-only access to it is permitted by applicable law and the venue's terms and is verified from the environment without any workaround. A venue whose access or eligibility is unresolved stays disabled for that operation, with its status recorded, and public access is never taken as permission to trade. Before any later trading activation, the account's actual eligibility, supported instruments, and jurisdiction, and every applicable risk and readiness gate, are verified. EXA-007 keeps its text: the owner's answer applies it to each operation, a venue being enabled for public market data where that access is permitted and verified, and for anything done with the operator's account only where the account is permitted, verified before that activation; PLT-012 keeps gating live trading. D1's other point stands as the plan states it, within the overall technical direction the owner approved: public market data is monitored for PLT-011's three instrument types where a venue publishes them (OPP-009). The builder asks the owner only for owner-level information that is genuinely missing.
4. **D2: infrastructure readiness.** Before any implementation that depends on infrastructure, a focused environment-readiness check, at U1, determines the smallest compliant solution with the approved technology stack and the infrastructure already available. Where the environment needed cannot be provided, the builder reports the exact limitation, the safest viable alternative, its dependencies, and any decision or expense that needs the owner's approval; it does not attempt again a configuration known to be unavailable, and buys no infrastructure without the owner's approval. D2's other points stand as the plan states them, since the requirements set them (PERF-006, PERF-018, SR-41, RSK-034, RSK-039): each baseline is labelled with its environment and is a first baseline only, and V-08 and V-17 stay "Not set" until the owner sets them.
5. **The owner's other conditions are existing rules, which the plan applies:** Stage 2 strictly read-only, with no live trading, private account access, capital deployment, or order execution (RMP-011; the plan's section 5); exact numbers, validated market data, clock synchronization, deterministic analysis, extensible regime detection, and continuous monitoring (its sections 3 and 4); no opportunity discarded only because its apparent profit percentage is small (SR-48; TNP-005; TNP-016; OPP-022; D10); and the three gates, security, testing, traceability, root-cause correction, and recovery rules, with no duplicate functionality or governance and no rework of approved parts the changes do not affect.
6. **Authorization.** Once this decision's change has passed its three gates, Stage 2's implementation is authorized, checkpoint by checkpoint, in the plan's order (its section 11), beginning with checkpoint A and U1's readiness check. It does not authorize bypassing an unresolved entry condition, buying infrastructure without the owner's approval, or weakening a technical requirement, and it covers Stage 2 only (constitution Rule 136).

## Alternatives considered

- **The plan as recommended, D1 and D2 included.** Not chosen: the owner approved it with changes to them.
- **D1 as the plan recommended it, the owner confirming which venues the account may use.** Not chosen: the owner keeps all five as candidates without declaring the account authorized on each, and has their public access verified per venue instead.
- **Starting Stage 2's work in the environment as observed on 2026-11-17.** Not chosen: the owner asks for a readiness check first, and for a report with alternatives where the environment cannot be provided.

- The plan's status becomes APPROVED; the roadmap's row for DATA FOUNDATION and its feature-status table, the project state, and the decision log follow.
- Stage 2's checkpoint A comes next, as its own change through the three gates, beginning with U1's readiness check.
- No requirement, rule, gate, or code changes with this record.
